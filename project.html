Case study · Logistics & Warehousing, Automotive Sector

An Interactive Health & Safety Training Platform

Replacing a static safety handbook with a scenario-based, 360° digital training experience — designed for warehouse staff who need to learn hazards fast, and remember them under pressure.

360°Walkthrough format
3Core training modules
VRStretch-goal extension
AgileSprint-based delivery

The brief

Why the client came to us

New warehouse staff have to understand hazard zones, PPE requirements and emergency procedures before starting work on site. The client's existing induction material was static and easy to skim past — which is exactly the problem on a floor where forklifts, loading bays and automotive stock all share the same space.

They needed something staff would actually engage with, not just sign to say they'd read.

Our approach

How we're solving it

We started with user requirements analysis — PACT and heuristic analysis against the people who'd actually use the system — before writing a single functional requirement. System requirements were mapped with structured flowcharts so the build stays traceable back to a real need, not a guess.

From there, the platform is being built in short, focused sprints, with a client review at the end of every cycle.

What the platform does

Five building blocks

Hazard-zone walkthrough

A 360° view of the warehouse floor that flags hazard zones as staff move through it, tied to real site layout.

PPE requirement checks

Role- and area-specific PPE requirements, tested as staff move between zones — not one generic list.

Emergency-procedure scenarios

Branching scenarios that test the right response to an incident, rather than a checklist to memorise.

Supervisor progress tracking

A simple dashboard so supervisors can see who's completed training and where they struggled.

VR extension (stretch goal)

A VR-based version of the walkthrough, scoped once the core web platform is validated with the client.

Built-in security review

Every module goes through our QA & security lead before it reaches the client — see our security approach on request.

Delivery roadmap

From brief to client presentation

Phase 01

Discovery & client introduction

An introductory session and initial requirements gathering, so the brief is understood before anything gets designed.

Phase 02

Requirements & design documentation

Functional and non-functional requirements, PACT/heuristic analysis, structured flowcharts.

Phase 03

Prototype build

First working increment of the training platform, ready to demo.

Phase 04

Client presentation

A focused session demonstrating the prototype and gathering structured feedback.

Phase 05

Iteration, QA & handover

Feedback-driven refinement, security review, and a plain-English user guide for the client.
