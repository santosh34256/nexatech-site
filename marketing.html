Zero-budget marketing plan

Winning clients without spending a penny.

Our finalised plan: who we are talking to, what we say, where we say it, and how we'll know it's working. Every line item costs £0.

1 · Situation & principle

Proof over promotion

As a new five-person student software studio we cannot outspend an agency, so we don't try. Our strongest marketing asset is real, documented work: a live client brief, a working training-platform prototype, and this website. Every channel below uses things we already have.

Positioning statement

For logistics and warehousing teams that need new starters trained safely and quickly, NexaTech Solution builds interactive, scenario-based training and clear promotional websites — delivered by a small, accountable team that documents everything it does.

2 · SMART objectives

What we aim to achieve in 12 weeks

10qualified enquiries via the website form
12LinkedIn progress posts, one per week
30personalised outreach emails (about 3 a week)
2referral introductions from current contacts

Targets are planning goals set by the team, reviewed at each weekly meeting.

3 · Target audience

Who we are talking to

Primary

Logistics & warehousing SMEs

Operations and health-and-safety managers who induct new staff and are tired of handbooks that get signed and forgotten. Pain point: slow, inconsistent, hard-to-evidence induction.

Primary

Automotive supply-chain firms

Parts storage and distribution sites with forklifts, COSHH materials and shared walkways — the exact zones our demo covers.

Secondary

Small businesses needing a web presence

Owners who need a clear, mobile-first promotional site that carries their identity properly, on a small budget.

Secondary

Training providers & colleges

Organisations looking for interactive scenario-based learning content for practical safety courses.

4 · Channels

Eight free channels, one message

Core message: safety-first software, built clearly and documented honestly.

Channel

LinkedIn & professional network

Weekly posts showing real progress — sprint reviews, design decisions, screenshots of the demo — from the company page and each member's profile.

Channel

This website

Always-on shop window: services, case study, downloadable demo and a working enquiry form. Every other channel links back here.

Channel

Client referral

A good outcome for our current client becomes the reference case. We ask for an introduction at project hand-over.

Channel

Team portfolios & GitHub

Individual ePortfolios and repositories evidence real contributions and point visitors to the studio site.

Channel

Direct email outreach

Short, specific emails to named managers at local logistics and automotive firms, with a link to the live demo.

Channel

Industry events & showcases

Present the platform at any available showcase or client presentation slot — free reach to a credible audience.

Channel

Search (SEO)

Clear page titles, descriptions, sitemap and semantic headings so the site can be found for “interactive warehouse safety training”. Registered free with Google Search Console.

Channel

College & alumni network

Tutors, the University of Sunderland community and placement contacts can share and introduce us to employers.

5 · Content calendar

Twelve weeks, three phases

Weeks 1–4 · Foundation

Publish the site and register it with Search Console. Set up the company LinkedIn page and portfolios. Post the studio introduction, the brand story and a case-study teaser. Build a list of 30 target firms.

Weeks 5–8 · Outreach

Send the first 3 personalised emails each week with the demo link. Post a short screen recording of the three training zones. Share a lessons-learned post from client feedback. Ask the current client for an introduction.

Weeks 9–12 · Proof & follow-up

Follow up every email once. Publish the finished case study and the supervisor-dashboard walkthrough. Present at any showcase slot. Review results against objectives and plan the next cycle.

8 · Risks & responses

What could go wrong

  • Low reply rate: test subject lines, narrow to firms with visible induction needs.
  • No time during deadlines: post from a pre-written queue; one owner per channel.
  • Prototype mistaken for finished product: always label it as a prototype and state the future-phase scope.
  • Inconsistent branding: everyone works from the brand page.

9 · Consistency

One identity, everywhere

The shield mark, navy-and-blue palette and plain tone of voice appear identically on this site, our deck, our poster and our documents, so each touchpoint reinforces the same studio rather than five separate efforts.
