How it works

Three zones. One clear journey.

Every zone follows the same three steps — explore, PPE check, knowledge check — so a trainee only has to learn the interface once.

Loading Bay

Reversing vehicles & dock plates

Hazard markers on a real dock layout — vehicle reversing zones, dock plate safety, and safe stacking limits.

Forklift Aisle

Pedestrian routes & blind corners

Walkway discipline, blind-corner mirrors, and racking load limits — the everyday hazards of a shared-traffic aisle.

Automotive Parts Storage

Manual handling & COSHH

Two-person lift procedure, controlled-substance storage, and keeping fire exits clear under delivery pressure.

The trainee journey

From walkthrough to a completed record

Step 1

Explore the 360° scene

Drag, swipe or use the arrow keys to look around the work area and open every hazard marker.

Step 2

Pass the PPE check

Choose exactly the PPE a task needs. Missing or unnecessary items are explained, never just marked wrong.

Step 3

Knowledge check

Three scenario questions per zone with an explanation after every answer, and unlimited retries.

Step 4

Emergency scenarios & final assessment

Branching decisions for a fire, a chemical spill and a forklift incident, then a randomised final assessment.

Step 5

Certificate & supervisor view

A printable certificate with a verifiable ID, and a dashboard with filters, drill-down, hazard reports and CSV export.

Platform features

Everything a safety lead needs, in one place

Built from the client brief: hazard-zone walkthroughs, PPE requirement checks, emergency-procedure scenarios and supervisor progress tracking.

Hazard zones

Draggable 360° scenes

Illustrated panoramas of the loading bay, forklift aisle and parts store, with hazard markers and a mini-map.

PPE checks

Task-based PPE selection

Trainees choose the PPE a real task needs and learn why each item is, or is not, required.

Emergencies

Branching scenarios

Fire, chemical spill and forklift incident — every decision scored with instant, explained feedback.

Assessment

Final test & certificate

Randomised questions, a configurable pass mark and a printable certificate with a verifiable ID.

Supervisors

Dashboard & hazard reports

Filters, drill-down per trainee, needs-attention flags, CSV export and a near-miss reporting log.

Administration

Edit content, stay in control

Change zone content, questions, pass mark and emergency contacts, with backup/restore and an activity log.

Also built in: text-size and high-contrast controls, keyboard-operable scenes, salted password hashing, sign-in lockout, automatic sign-out after inactivity and achievement badges. A VR headset version is scoped as a later phase.

Why this approach

Built to be used, not just signed off

A printed handbook gets signed and forgotten. Our platform is built around interaction — dragging, clicking, answering — because that's what actually holds attention on a warehouse floor, based on the PACT and heuristic analysis behind this project.

Every design decision is documented, not assumed: see our full case study for how the brief shaped the build.
