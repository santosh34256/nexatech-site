Current client brief

An Interactive Health & Safety Training Platform

Our client is a logistics and warehousing company in the automotive sector. New warehouse staff need to understand hazard zones, PPE requirements and emergency procedures before they start work — and reading a PDF handbook isn't cutting it.

We're building an interactive, scenario-based training experience to replace it, with a VR-based extension scoped as a stretch goal once the core platform is proven.
