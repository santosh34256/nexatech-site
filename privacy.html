Privacy & accessibility

How this website treats your data.

Short and plain: what we collect, what we don't, and how we've made the site usable for everyone.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no analytics scripts. The only information you can give us is through the contact form: your name, email, optional company and phone number, the service you're interested in, and your message.

Where your enquiry goes

  • When you submit the form, a copy is kept only in your own browser (local storage) so you can download a receipt.
  • To actually reach us, the form then offers to open a pre-filled email to hello@nexatechsolution.dev in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app.
  • We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and do not share them with third parties.

Clearing your data

You can remove the locally saved enquiries at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The interactive training demo has its own “Reset demo data” button.

Training demo

The downloadable training demo is a prototype. Accounts, progress and edits live in your browser only. Please do not enter real employee data.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts; your browser may contact Google to fetch them. If blocked, the site falls back to system fonts.

Accessibility

  • Skip-to-content link and keyboard-operable navigation with visible focus states.
  • Semantic headings and landmarks, labelled form fields with inline error messages.
  • Responsive layout from phone to desktop; respects “reduce motion” settings.
  • A high-contrast mode in the training demo.

If you find something hard to use, email us and we will fix it.
